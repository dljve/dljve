## 🧭 My journey in AI

My interest in AI started with a question about people: *how do we make sense of the world, and can a machine learn to do the same?* That question took me through a bachelor's and master's in Artificial Intelligence at Radboud University, with courses in cognitive psychology alongside machine learning.

- **BSc Artificial Intelligence** (Radboud University, 2012–2016)
  My thesis explored whether a neural network could learn human moral judgements about *care and harm* from survey data, a bottom-up approach to artificial moral agents. Grade: 8.5.

- **MSc Artificial Intelligence**, Intelligent Technology (Radboud University, 2017–2021, graduated with an 8.0)
  For my thesis at the **Radboudumc Diagnostic Image Analysis Group** I built a deep learning system (U-Net) that detects the abdominal aorta in handheld ultrasound and measures its diameter, running in **real time on a smartphone**. The goal: letting non-specialists screen for abdominal aortic aneurysms outside the hospital. Grade: 8.5.

- **MSc Computer Science**, Data Science (Radboud University)
  Coursework completed. During an internship at **Thirona** I researched **self-supervised learning (SimCLR)** on chest X-rays for tuberculosis detection, showing that pre-trained representations can cut the number of labels needed roughly in half.

- **Software Engineer at Binding Blocks** (2024–2025)
  Built an educational algebra and calculus game with symbolic equation solving and a step-by-step hint system ([bindingblocks.nl](https://bindingblocks.nl)). I also built the math learning platform [Wisgame](https://wisgame.nl).

## ❤️ What drives me

My preference has always been **medical AI**: models that give a clinician a fast, reliable second opinion, or bring diagnostics to places where specialists are scarce. But I love **every application where the human is at the centre**: education, language, wellbeing, and making AI understandable instead of a black box.
